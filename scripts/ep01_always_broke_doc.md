# Tập 1: Why You're Always Broke (Even With a Good Salary)

**Tiêu đề (nghĩa):** Vì sao bạn luôn hết tiền (dù lương khá)

**4 tiêu đề (xếp hạng):**
1. Why You're Always Broke (Even With a Good Salary)
2. $3,400 a Month and Still Broke: The 4 Hidden Leaks
3. Where Your Paycheck Really Goes (The Math Nobody Shows You)
4. Ben Makes $3,400 a Month. He Has $33 Left. Here's Why.

**Mô tả (nghĩa):** Vì sao bạn luôn hết tiền dù lương khá? Video lần theo từng đồng trong 3.400 đô lương của Ben, tìm 4 lỗ rò rút cạn nó, và cách sửa quan trọng nhất: trả cho bản thân trước.

**Số liệu:** Ben's monthly take-home pay $3,400 (example budget). Car: $28,000 loan at 7.9% APR for 6 years = $490/month, $7,249 total interest; a $15,000 car on the same terms = $262/month, $3,883 interest. Burger: $14 menu price + $2 app markup + $3 service fee + $3 delivery fee + $4 tip = $26 (example). Card: $4,000 at 24% APR paying $120/month, no new spending = 56 months, $2,658 interest. Small stuff: $312 + $96 + $87 = $495/month, $5,940/year. Investing: $200/month at a 7% average yearly return, compounded monthly, 30 years = about $243,994 ($72,000 put in). Examples, not promises; returns vary and can be negative.
