## Never
- Specific buy/sell calls (stocks, crypto coins), price predictions, "get rich quick", income promises
  ("make $10K/month passive"): misleading, policy risk, kills trust.
- Real people's finances or real companies' internal numbers you cannot source.
- Medical, legal or tax advice for a personal situation. Explain the concept, point to a professional.
